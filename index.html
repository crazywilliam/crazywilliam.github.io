💡 About Me

I am Weimin Wang (William), an MSc graduate in Statistics and Data Science at Leiden University (GPA: 8.28/10).

My research interests lie at the intersection of statistical learning, machine learning, and explainable AI. I am particularly interested in developing reliable and interpretable machine learning methods for complex real-world systems.

My recent work focuses on data-enabled predictive control, statistical modeling, causal inference, and deep learning. I am currently seeking PhD opportunities in machine learning and artificial intelligence.
